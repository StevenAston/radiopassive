# Stream 3, oil and gas: annotated bibliography (Task 1.4)

IDs S0300–S0333 (34 sources; full records in `sources_task14.csv`). Existing UNSCEAR sources S0001, S0005 and S0008 are cited by ID and not repeated. Conversions: 1 pCi = 0.037 Bq; 1 pCi/L = 37 Bq/m³; 1 pCi/g = 0.037 Bq/g. Rank follows the source hierarchy (1 regulator, 2 compilation, 3 peer reviewed, 4 agency, 5 grey).

## A. Primary sources (24 listed; 22 by source type)

### Produced water: radium by play

- **S0300 Rowan et al. 2011, USGS SIR 2011-5135** (agency, open). Ra-226, Ra-228 and total radium in Appalachian produced water, NY and PA, 1990s–2010. Marcellus total Ra median 2,460 pCi/L (91 Bq/L), maximum 18,000 pCi/L; non-Marcellus median 734 (abstract) or 1,011 pCi/L (text), a flagged internal inconsistency. Ra-228/Ra-226 median 0.16 Marcellus, 1.1 otherwise. Quality high; Appalachia only.
- **S0301 PA DEP TENORM Study 2016** (regulator, open). 2013–14 sampling in Pennsylvania: produced water, flowback, frac fluid, cuttings, filter cake, wellhead gas radon, gas-plant Pb-210. The best single source for fracked wells. Caveat: the file is a tracked-changes draft, so use the tables and not the prose. Small n (9 unconventional produced-water samples).
- **S0302 / S0303 USGS NPWGD v2.3 and v3.0** (agency, open). Database used to compute play statistics (task computation, not USGS-published). Ra-226 in 812 records and Ra-228 in 237 in v3.0 out of 113,135. Adds Bakken (n=17), Utica and road-spreading brine in v3.0. No Permian, Eagle Ford, Gulf of Mexico, North Sea or Middle East radium. Non-detects often stored as 0.
- **S0316 Xu et al. 2022, NM WRRI TR 398** (grey, open). Only Permian radium found (46 samples, New Mexico): Ra-226 mean 1.6 Bq/L, Ra-228 mean 5.6 Bq/L, Ra-228 exceeds Ra-226. Cites Scanlon et al. (S0315) per-play total-Ra medians. Small, regional, one operator area.
- **S0324 McDevitt et al. 2019** (peer reviewed). Wyoming onshore surface discharges: Ra-226 0.03–1.24 Bq/L; shows the discharge route is a small activity flux.
- **S0308 Schulz and Varskog 2012 (Zpire 03-12)** (grey, open). Ten Norwegian installations: Ra-226 0.5–10, Ra-228 0.5–8.7, Pb-210 mostly <0.2 Bq/L; NCS 2011 totals of 129 Mm³ water, 451 GBq Ra-226, 356 GBq Ra-228. States routine Pb-210 reporting overstates by at least a factor of 10.

### Volumes and disposal routes

- **S0311 Veil 2019** (grey) and **S0312 GWPC 2020** (inaccessible). US produced water 20.2 (2007), 21.2 (2012), 24.4 Gbbl (2017); 2017 routes EOR 44.0%, injection disposal 37.9%, commercial 9.9%, discharge 5.5%, reuse 2.3%, evaporation 0.4%. Slides read through a summarising tool; verify against S0312.
- **S0313 Clark and Veil 2009** (inaccessible). 2007 baseline, not retrieved.
- **S0314 IOGP EPI 2018** (grey). Member-company produced water: 814 Mt discharged, 1,107 Mt re-injected (2018). Not a world total.
- **S0315 Scanlon et al. 2020** (paywalled; summary read). Unconventional produced water about 600 billion L in 2017.
- **S0332 Texas Living Waters/EDF 2025** and **S0333 Khatib** (grey). Permian Texas 12 Mbbl/d; global water-oil ratio above 3 and rising. Advocacy and promotional slide material; plausibility anchors only.

### Offshore discharges (measured)

- **S0307 OSPAR 2018 non-nuclear report** (regulator, open). Annual Ra-226 discharge 0.73–1.1 TBq/a, 2005–2018; the only directly reported annual activity series for the stream. 2018 Ra-228 figure differs between text and table. Reports for 2019–2024 not read.

### Scale, sludge, gas-side

- **S0304 IAEA SRS 34** (compilation). Range table (Table III) for oil, gas, water, scale and sludge; melting behaviour of scrap.
- **S0305 IAEA TRS 419** (compilation). Residue activity ranges in Bq/kg. Quoted 100 t of scale per well per year is not credible; do not use.
- **S0306 IAEA TRS 474** (compilation). Radon emanation method and generic coefficients.
- **S0321 White and Rood 1999** (agency). Rn-222 emanation fractions of oil-field scale 0.037–0.138 and contaminated soil 0.09–0.21. The only oil-field-specific ε input. Emanation is not effective exhalation.
- **S0309, S0310 Varskog et al.** (grey). Norwegian sludge and scale activity, waste quantities, Stangeneset disposal.
- **S0320 USGS FS-142-99, S0328 EPA TENORM page** (agency). Scale average 17.8 Bq/g, sludge 2.8 Bq/g; API 1989 survey; scrap-dealer discovery. Secondary compilations of 1980s–90s US data.
- **S0327 Xhixha et al. 2013** (peer reviewed). Scale and sludge by country including the only Middle East values (Syria 147–1,050 Bq/g); some rows suspect (units).
- **S0317 Rowan and Kraemer 2012, S0318 Mitchell et al. 2016, S0319 Gray 1993** (agency, peer reviewed, grey). Radon in natural gas: Appalachian measurements and the historical compilation of Johnson 1973 (mean 1,370 Bq/m³, range 7–54,000). Complement S0001 (UNSCEAR 625 Bq/m³ mean).

### Early history and cuttings

- **S0329 Gott and Hill 1953** (agency). Earliest quantitative record: Ra-bearing precipitates in more than 60 Kansas oil and gas fields. Basis for the pre-1950 stock approach.
- **S0330 Bank 2011** (grey) and **S0331 NDDoH/Argonne** (regulator). Shale uranium 8–53 ppm; North Dakota says Bakken cuttings are within the natural soil range.

## B. Environmental release and event-candidate sources

- **S0322 Côté 2017** (grey thesis): North Dakota spills (about 3,900 in 2007–2014); Blacktail Creek about 11 million L; Bear Den Bay 3.8 million L. Leads to Lauer et al. 2016.
- **S0323 Van Sice et al. 2018**, **S0325 Tasker et al. 2018** (paywalled, press figures only): Pennsylvania CWT discharge history and road-spreading releases.
- **S0326 AER/AGS INF 157** (regulator): oil sands ore, froth-treatment tailings and monazite quantities.
