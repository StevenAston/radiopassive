# Coal (stream 2): annotated bibliography, Task 1.3

IDs S0200-S0254 (55 sources, full records in `sources_task13.csv`). UNSCEAR sources S0001, S0006, S0008, S0010 are cited by ID only (see `research/unscear_notes.md`). "Read" means the text or data were opened; "lead" means found but not read.

## A. Primary sources actually read (22)

- **S0200 USGS FS-163-97, Radioactive Elements in Coal and Fly Ash.** US coal U 1-4 ppm, Th 1-4 ppm; fly ash U 10-30 ppm; mean ash yield about 10%. Stage combustion_power. Quality: authoritative agency fact sheet, 1997, US only, mass units (convert 1 mg/kg U = 12.35 Bq/kg).
- **S0202 IAEA TRS 419, Radiation protection and NORM residue management in the production of coal.** Country tables for coal, ash, gangue (China gangue Ra-226 53, Th-232 61, K-40 524 Bq/kg, n=643), enhancement factors (Table X: Pb-210 20-120, Po-210 30-200 in ash particles), Brazil/Croatia/Poland/Hungary/Egypt/UK ash. Quality: high (IAEA, 2003), compilation of older data; waste rock mass qualitative.
- **S0203 IAEA TRS 474, Measurement and calculation of radon releases from NORM residues.** Emanation: coal fly ash generally below 0.1; framework for epsilon. Quality: high; no coal-specific measurements beyond reviews.
- **S0204 EC RP 135, Radiological impact of NORM in the EU (coal chapters).** EU consumption by use, ash and FGD gypsum tonnages (1999), ash yield. Quality: regulator-grade compilation, 2003.
- **S0205 NRPB UK paper (UK coal-fired power).** UK coal 15 Bq/kg U-chain, ash 16% (10-20%), 7.4 Mt ash and 30 kt emitted (1998). Year inferred 2001. Quality: good, UK only.
- **S0207 EPA (Watson), US particulate emissions 1970.** Control efficiency by sector (utilities 78%, ESP 83%, mechanical 70%, residential 60%, industrial 80%). Year (1974) inferred. Quality: agency, snapshot.
- **S0208 Smith and Gruber 1966, Atmospheric emissions from coal combustion.** Fly/bottom partition by firing type; collector efficiencies by device (ESP 85-99%, multicyclone 75-90%). Key for pre-1960 firing regimes. Quality: US government report, 1966; US technology only.
- **S0209 CEA India, fly ash generation and utilisation reports.** Fly ash 68.9 Mt (1996-97) to 232.6 Mt (2020-21), utilisation modes. Quality: regulator, annual; ash content 33.9%.
- **S0210 Heidrich et al. 2013.** Global CCP 780 Mt (2010), 53% utilised; 80-85% fly ash. Quality: peer-reviewed industry review.
- **S0211 ECOBA/WOCA statistics.** EU-15 CCP 1993-2010, disposition, FGD gypsum. Titles unconfirmed. Quality: industry grey.
- **S0212 Jow, China coal ash.** China 301 Mt (2005) to 520 Mt (2013). Quality: grey/trade.
- **S0213 US EPA CCR rule.** US 110 Mt, 40% used (2012); 735 impoundments, 310 landfills. Quality: regulator.
- **S0215 Concrete Products (ACAA survey).** US 66.7 Mt (2023), 63.6 Mt (2024), 72% used. Quality: trade reporting of ACAA data.
- **S0216 Lauer et al. 2015, ES&T 49:11227.** US coals Ra 28-39 Bq/kg; CCRs 213-297 Bq/kg; enrichment 7-10x. Quality: peer-reviewed, best modern US ash data.
- **S0220 Chen et al. 2017.** China coal U 2.43 mg/kg (n=1883) and world 2.40 mg/kg (Ketris and Yudovich) by citation. Quality: secondary; primary papers paywalled.
- **S0221 Karangelos, NORM III (Greece lignite).** Lignite Ra-226 346 Bq/kg; fly ash Ra-226 904, Pb-210 1158; ash-field exhalation 2-24 mBq/m2/s. Year inferred. Quality: peer-reviewed proceedings, one country.
- **S0222 Chalupnik and Wysocka 2007; S0224, S0225, S0226.** Upper Silesian mine water: Ra-226 releases 400 (1987) to 77 (2006) MBq/d, Ra-228, waters to 390 kBq/m3, BaSO4 deposits to 400 kBq/kg, sediments median 409 Bq/kg (n=39). Contradiction on retention (over 70% vs about 40%). Quality: good measurements, Poland only.
- **S0227 Trotti, coke-oven residues (Italy).** Only coke-oven data: dust Pb-210 14, Po-210 2; tar 181/177 Bq/kg. Quality: single plant.
- **S0230 OWID / Energy Institute (Etemad and Luciani; Smil).** World coal TWh from 1800 (97; 569 in 1850; 5350 in 1900; 11333 in 1950); country series. Quality: compilation; energy units; US/Germany/China start 1900.
- **S0231 UK DESNZ, Historical coal data 1853-.** Tonnes and consumption by sector; electricity share 2.5% (1913), 8.4% (1938), 15% (1949). Quality: official, best long UK series.
- **S0232 EIA, Annual Energy Review table 6.2.** US coal by sector from 1949; power 17.4% (1949), 44% (1960), 81% (1980). Quality: official.
